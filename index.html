Transparency
Data policy & privacy
Facade Studio is designed to generate prompts in your browser.

    

      
Your prompt and settings
Facade Studio does not send or save the prompt text, selected colors, or custom instructions to a Facade Studio server. These settings are handled in your browser. When you copy or download a prompt, that action is performed by your browser.

      
Google Analytics
This site uses Google Analytics to understand general website usage and improve the experience. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect usage and device information. Analytics data is processed by Google under its applicable terms and privacy practices.

      
What this means
Do not include sensitive or confidential information in custom instructions. This page does not claim that Google Analytics collects no data. For details about Google’s data practices and available controls, review Google’s privacy information and your browser’s cookie settings.
